# FINAL ROADMAP
# AI-Powered Product Intelligence Platform
## UniHack Final Blueprint (2-Member Team)

============================================================
VISION
============================================================

Build an adaptive AI-powered Product Intelligence Platform that transforms scattered product information into trusted, explainable, commerce-ready intelligence.

The platform automatically adapts itself to different companies and industries using AI-driven company discovery, industry profiles, dynamic schemas, dynamic validation rules and dynamic UI rendering.

The first implementation focuses on ONE industrial vertical for the hackathon, while the architecture is extensible to multiple industries.
